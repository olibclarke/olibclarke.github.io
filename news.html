Oliver named VP&S Schaefer Scholar

Columbia Anesthesiology News

Dr. Oliver B. Clarke has been named a 2026 Schaefer Research Scholar by the Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons (VP&S). This prestigious scholarship recognizes and supports research that successfully bridges clinical relevance and basic scientific inquiry, funding his ongoing research on the structural biology of membrane proteins.

May 2026

High-Resolution Ab Initio Reconstruction Featured on CryoSPARC Blog

CryoSPARC Blog

Our method development work for small and challenging macromolecular complexes, HR-HAIR (High-Resolution Heterogeneous Ab Initio Reconstruction), authored by Kookjoo, Huan, and Oli, is highlighted in an in-depth technical showcase on the official CryoSPARC blog. The article details our algorithms and protocols for achieving high-resolution ab initio 3D reconstructions from single-particle cryo-EM data.
